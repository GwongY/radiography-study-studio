Muscle zoom (outputs/muscle/, outputs/studio/muscle-zoom.js)
No third-party asset is added. The whole-muscle level shows the viewer's own muscle layer, BodyParts3D /
Anatomography (c) DBCLS, CC BY-SA 2.1 JP, as above. The fascicle, muscle fiber, myofibril, sarcomere and
neuromuscular junction levels are geometry generated in three.js by this app (outputs/muscle/zoom-scene.js).

2026-10-20; none could be fetched and licence-checked from the build environment, and the one sarcomere mesh found
by search (Sketchfab "Sarcomere Model", rgmz2015) is CC BY-NC, which this repository does not use.
