Cell zoom (outputs/cell/, outputs/cell-zoom.js, outputs/studio/cell-zoom.js)
The nucleosome level is the Protein Data Bank entry PDB 3AFA, a human nucleosome (two 146-nucleotide DNA strands
and eight histones): https://www.rcsb.org/structure/3AFA
Authors: Tachiwana H, Kagawa W, Osakabe A, Kawaguchi K, Shiga T, Hayashi-Takanaka Y, Kimura H, Kurumizaka H.
Primary citation: Proc Natl Acad Sci USA 2010;107:10454-10459.
Licence: data files in the PDB archive are available under CC0 1.0 Universal (RCSB PDB usage policy,
https://www.rcsb.org/pages/usage-policy, checked 2026-10-20). The same structure is NIH 3D entry 3DPX-002662
(public domain), https://3d.nih.gov/entries/3DPX-002662. outputs/cell/nucleosome-3afa.js keeps, per nucleotide, its
base, one backbone atom and C1', and each histone's C-alpha trace, reduced by work/build-cell-nucleosome.mjs from
the 3AFA mmCIF file; nothing else of the entry is redistributed.
Each level opens as a drawing generated in three.js by this app, then wears a 3D model (outputs/cell/models/*.glb,
outputs/cell/models.js) built in Blender by work/build-cell-models.mjs and work/blender/. The models use these

Changes made to every download: simplified (fewer triangles), textures shrunk, cut into the parts the app labels
and recoloured to them, scaled and placed in the level's frame; parts of some were reshaped to the slide's
proportions (the spermatozoon's head, neck, middle piece and tail; the neuron's myelinated axon), made see-through
(cells whose insides are shown), or set moving with the level's stage slider. What the downloads lack — organelles,
membrane proteins, junctions, the nucleus and DNA levels, the life cycle, meiosis, protein synthesis and the
hormone signals, the parietal cell, the perforin and MHC proteins, the nucleosome's surfaces from PDB 3AFA — is
modelled in Blender for this app. Sketchfab models that were found but are CC BY-NC, or are photographs of a
physical model with no separable parts ("Mitosis Stage 1"…"9" by jkane326, CC BY 4.0), are not used.
