Muscle zoom (outputs/muscle/, outputs/studio/muscle-zoom.js)
No third-party asset is added. The whole-muscle level shows the viewer's own muscle layer, BodyParts3D /
Anatomography (c) DBCLS, CC BY-SA 2.1 JP, as above. The fascicle, muscle fiber, myofibril, sarcomere and
neuromuscular junction levels are geometry generated in three.js by this app (outputs/muscle/zoom-scene.js).
Openly licensed meshes were looked for first (Sketchfab, NIH 3D, Wikimedia Commons, Smithsonian 3D, RCSB PDB) on
2026-10-20; none could be fetched and licence-checked from the build environment, and the one sarcomere mesh found
by search (Sketchfab "Sarcomere Model", rgmz2015) is CC BY-NC, which this repository does not use.

Cell zoom (outputs/cell/, outputs/cell-zoom.js, outputs/studio/cell-zoom.js)
The nucleosome level is the Protein Data Bank entry PDB 3AFA, a human nucleosome (two 146-nucleotide DNA strands
and eight histones): https://www.rcsb.org/structure/3AFA
Authors: Tachiwana H, Kagawa W, Osakabe A, Kawaguchi K, Shiga T, Hayashi-Takanaka Y, Kimura H, Kurumizaka H.
Primary citation: Proc Natl Acad Sci USA 2010;107:10454-10459.
Licence: data files in the PDB archive are available under CC0 1.0 Universal (RCSB PDB usage policy,
https://www.rcsb.org/pages/usage-policy, checked 2026-10-20). The same structure is NIH 3D entry 3DPX-002662
(public domain), https://3d.nih.gov/entries/3DPX-002662. outputs/cell/nucleosome-3afa.js keeps, per nucleotide, its
base, one backbone atom and C1', and each histone's C-alpha trace, reduced by work/build-cell-nucleosome.mjs from
the 3AFA mmCIF file; nothing else of the entry is redistributed.
Every other level (the cell, nucleus, chromatin and chromosome, life cycle, meiosis, protein synthesis and the
hormone signals, the plasma membrane, epithelium, blood, alveolus, parietal cell, spermatozoon, neuron, NK cell and
antigen-presenting cell), and the nucleosome's linker DNA and neighbours, is geometry generated in three.js by this
app. Openly licensed models were looked for first on 2026-10-20 (NIH 3D, Sketchfab, RCSB PDB); the NIH 3D model
files could not be downloaded from the build environment and Sketchfab downloads need an account, so no other
third-party asset is added.
