| Radiation protection principles, measures and limits: Four radioprotective measures: (1) Time: minimize exposure duration (dose-rate issue), (2) Distance: maximize distance from source governed by inverse square law ($I \propto 1/d^2$, double distance = quarter dose), (3) Shielding: lead aprons, thyroid shields, mobile lead screens, structural bunker shielding, (4) Decay: allow natural radionuclide decay for unremovable radioactive material governed by half-life (physical, biological, effective); ALARA principle (As Low As Reasonably Achievable); ICRP Publication 103 dose limits: radiation workers (20 mSv/year averaged over 5 consecutive years, 50 mSv single year max, 1 mSv declared pregnancy), public limit (1 mSv/year); personnel monitoring: Thermoluminescent Dosimeter (TLD, lithium fluoride crystal, detection range 0.05 mSv to 10 Sv, not feasible for real-time accidental alarms); biological radiation damage paradigm: stochastic effects (probabilistic, no threshold, cancer/genetic mutations) vs deterministic effects (tissue reactions, severity proportional to dose above threshold, cataracts, erythema, radiation sickness); exposure sources: medical diagnostic/interventional procedures account for >90% of artificial radiation exposure | hti.w6 p1–16 | Worksheet MI Q5; radioprotection principles & legal dose limits | **`hti17103-radioprotection`** | hti.w6 p3, 5, 8–16 |
