# Piece 5 — vessel and nerve activity on curved paths

Status: draft. Depends on 0 and 4. Parent: [decomposition](2026-09-09-physiology-decomposition.md).

## Change

Extend curved-path light activity to a curated set using piece 4's validated payload
and diagnostics. Preserve the established motor-route sequence and all other activity
cues. Ship a route inventory with accepted/rejected reasons; do not solve hundreds
of graphs synchronously during browser layer load.

For each route specify source, direction, origin region, component order and display
timing. Reuse scalar path progress for glow; no tube-radial displacement is needed in
this piece. A successful geodesic calculation only proves geometric reachability,
not anatomical flow direction. Do not use the highest vertex as a universal proximal
seed or create continuity across disconnected components without an explicit mapping.

## Physiological distinctions

Separate illustrative blood-flow motion from a pressure-pulse cue; a pressure wave
is not a packet of blood. Preserve pulmonary/systemic colour classification. Mixed
nerves must not silently be relabelled as exclusively motor or assigned universal
outward traffic. New linked nerve-muscle sequences need confirmed innervation and
source-backed ordering; unmatched nerves retain a clearly illustrative activity cue.

## Cost and failure behaviour

Stay within piece 0's per-layer transfer and resident-memory budgets. Reuse payloads
on visibility toggles; no recomputation per frame. Verify GLB/attribute/route hashes
before binding arrays. Test version mismatch, truncated payloads, repeat toggles,
offline loading and disposed/reloaded materials. Keep shader cache variants explicit
so curved and fallback materials cannot silently share the wrong shader program.

## Evidence and acceptance

Pure checks assert finite bounded progress, known ordered samples, component handling,
seam welding and deterministic output. Candidate curved vessels such as the aortic
arch require actual model/name validation and a justified origin; they are not
pre-approved by this document. Maintain an inventory of animated eligible meshes to
prove coverage does not shrink when a new profile is unavailable.

Browser checks compare full cycles on curved and fallback examples, verifying travel
direction visually against the curated route and testing the mapped motor delay.
No surface deformation or normal changes should occur for glow-only routes. Use
fixed views and lights so the effect cannot be confused with turntable motion.
Apply the parent performance/interaction gates and all classification regressions.

## Source gate and exclusions

Verify exact cardiovascular and neural claims in the supplied course material, then
extend MECHANISM_SOURCES and the profile claim ledger. Explicitly label slowed or
unquantified timing illustrative. No capillary network invention, measured conduction
velocity claim, full-body neural connectivity reconstruction or fluid simulation.
