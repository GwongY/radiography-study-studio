# Y1S1 concept coverage and teaching order

The user requests implementation, including adding, enriching, removing or regrouping
app lessons. The previous source map proves that references resolve; it does not prove
that the app teaches every concept in those files. Its `complete` status must never
be presented as syllabus completeness.

The latest supplied syllabus and schedule determine subjects, weeks and module labels.
Current New source teaching files have priority. Older official lectures can fill the
same current topic, with their provenance visible. Student work and administration do
not supply subject facts. No future semester is assigned without its current syllabus.

Learn and Course share a subject/week sequence derived from the current timetable.
Lessons follow teaching order. Cross-subject anatomy aids are optional prerequisites,
not physiology lessons. Sociology and named missing teaching weeks remain discoverable.
Storage IDs remain stable so regrouping preserves progress.

Each subject audit accounts for source pages and maps taught concepts to actual lesson
text and exact page references. A page citation or keyword alone cannot prove that its
concept is explained. Image-only or uninspected pages remain explicit review gaps.
Missing teaching files cannot be filled by inventing content from a syllabus heading.
Original files remain the source notes; enrichment changes the app teaching content.

Completion requires source citation checks, lesson/schedule consistency, concept-audit
checks, the repository verifier suite, and real Chrome acceptance of navigation,
teaching order, source provenance, gap visibility, and reading at tablet/desktop sizes.
