# Cell zoom — spec (2026-10-20)

## What

A Viewer mode, mainly for ABCT2326 (Lecture 1 Cells, the Cells tutorial, Reproduction's meiosis), reached two
ways: the **Cell** button in the Viewer's dock (beside Heart atlas and Limb vessels), and **Show in 3D** on a
Revise box about the same thing (`SHOW_IN_3D` in `outputs/cell-zoom.js`: 109 ABCT2326 boxes, 7 HSS2011 W5 neuron
boxes), which opens the level, the stage and the label that box is about.

Every level is in the panel's **Go to** menu, grouped by source. Every label is a numbered tag on the part and a row
in the list; tapping either (or the part in 3D) highlights the part and shows its one-line note in the slide's
words, its page, and "In Revise" links that follow Revise's week gate. A part a stage does not show has no tag and
a dimmed row.

## Sources

Labels and notes come only from the cited page (checked by `work/cell-zoom-check.mjs`, in `work/check.mjs`). The
2026 decks first; the tutorials (kind "assessment") for the mitosis stage names (Cells tutorial p3, Tutorial answers
p7 for anaphase); one named fallback, the older ABCT2326 Hormone Mechanism handout, for CREB and the hormone
response element, which the 2026 Endocrine deck does not reach; and an older edition of Lecture 1
(`phys.cells.old`, p33) for G1, S and G2, which the 2026 deck folds into "interphase". Drawn but unlabelled, because no source page uses
the word: base pair, tetrad, homologous, linker DNA, H2A/H2B/H3/H4. "DNA double helix" is labelled: L1 p14 says it.

## Assets

The nucleosome's data is PDB 3AFA (Tachiwana et al. 2010), CC0 per the RCSB usage policy, the same structure as
NIH 3D 3DPX-002662 (public domain). `work/build-cell-nucleosome.mjs <3AFA.cif>` reduces it to bases, one backbone
atom, C1' and C-alpha traces (19 KB), stamped with the source file's hash.

Every level opens as its procedural three.js drawing (`outputs/cell/`, about 90 KB of scene code), so it is
there at once and offline, and then **wears a 3D model** (2026-10-21, after the owner found the drawings buggy and
asked for online models, and for Blender for everything not found online): `outputs/cell/models/<level>.glb`,
listed in `outputs/cell/models.js`, 17 files, about 12 MB in all, each fetched when its level first opens and
cache-first after that. A model is a CC BY 4.0 Sketchfab download wherever one exists (the cell, centrosome, red
cells, lymphocyte, platelet, macrophage, neuron, spermatozoon, plasma membrane, tracheal epithelium and Chromosome
X; the owner signed in to download them), cut into the level's labelled parts; everything the downloads lack is
modelled in Blender: organelles and proteins added to them, and the nucleus, DNA, nucleosome (histone surfaces from
the 3AFA C-alpha traces), life cycle, meiosis, protein synthesis and parietal cell whole. A slider level is
animated in Blender with the drawing's own choreography (stage k from second k; the expression level's three
signals are three runs of one timeline, `czClips`). Build, three steps (headers say how):

```
node work/build-cell-models.mjs prepare --modules <node_modules> --src <unzipped downloads>
blender --background --factory-startup --python work/blender/build.py -- [--level a,b]
node work/build-cell-models.mjs finish --modules <node_modules> --write
```

The life cycle, meiosis, protein synthesis and parietal cell were remade on 2026-10-21 after the owner's review:
hollow 9 + 0 centrioles, spindle fibres computed to the kinetochores they pull, an eight-step life cycle (G1, S, G2,
the four phases of mitosis, cytokinesis), a double nuclear envelope with pore complexes, a helix of whole turns
wound twice round each histone core, ribosomes on the ER with the chain entering its lumen, and the parietal
cell's carbonic anhydrase reaction, pump, countertransporter and chloride channels as separate, labelled steps.

`finish` refuses a model whose nodes are not the level's parts, all of them, or a slider level with no animation.
Not used: the "Mitosis Stage" scans (photographs of a clay model in one texture, no part can be cut out) and any
CC BY-NC model. Credits: the panel's line, `MODEL_SOURCES` and `outputs/THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt`, held together
by `work/cell-zoom-check.mjs`.

## Mode rules

The fourth atlas mode (`state.heartAtlasOn` + `state.cellZoomOn`): an exclusive view, every layer off, a snapshot
taken on entry and given back on exit (layers, camera, distance limits). Lazy `import()` of
`cell/zoom-scene.js?v=1`, precached in the SW shell with its imports, so it opens offline once the app is installed.
Verified in Chromium at 390×844 and 820×1180: all 16 levels framed with their tags, every stage of every slider
and signal, Play to the end, tap-to-pick, exit restoring the layers, and an offline reload reopening the life cycle
at anaphase.
