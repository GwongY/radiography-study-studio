## 3. Recommendation & Evaluation of Options

### The Three Options:
- **Option (a) [RECOMMENDED]**: **Trim both lessons back to the sourced + tested core.**
  - *Plexus*: Roots (C5–T1 anterior/ventral rami) $\rightarrow$ the 5 major terminal nerves (Musculocutaneous, Axillary, Radial, Median, Ulnar), their motor compartments (arm flexors, deltoid, all extensors, forearm flexors/thenar, intrinsic hand), and the clinically tested Carpal Tunnel Syndrome (median nerve compression). Remove divisions, cord-artery positional relations, quadrangular space, radial groove, regimental badge, cubital tunnel, claw hand, and wrist drop.
  - *Femoral Triangle*: Location (anterior and superior thigh) $+$ the 3 boundaries (inguinal ligament, adductor longus, sartorius) $+$ the 3 contents (femoral nerve, femoral artery, femoral vein) $+$ the lower limb vascular continuity (External iliac $\rightarrow$ Femoral $\rightarrow$ Popliteal $\rightarrow$ Tibial / Great saphenous vein) confirmed in `hss.3.3`, `hss.4.3`, and Revision Exercise G. Remove NAVEL empty space/canal, femoral hernia, Cloquet's node, adductor canal/hiatus, and cribriform fascia.
- **Option (b)**: **Keep the depth but split every unsourced claim into an explicit app-authored block with its own tag**, similar to how prior-knowledge `beyond` lines are handled in visual callouts.
- **Option (c)**: **Leave as-is with rationale.**

### Detailed Rationale for Option (a):

1. **Strict Compliance with `CLAUDE.md` Hard Rules**:
   - `CLAUDE.md` explicitly mandates:
     > **"Source traceability."** *Every factual study claim must cite a sourceRefs entry keyed to a file that actually exists in the supplied source folders... No internet research, no generic textbook expansion, no invented syllabus.*
     > **"Taught & tested syllabus scope."** *Lessons must correspond strictly to real lecture topics, tutorial case studies/problems, and examination scope. No synthetic micro-lessons... and no unverified curriculum expansion.*
   - `outputs/README.md` explicitly specifies under *Deliberate content limits*:
     > *"Advanced muscle attachments, detailed vascular branches, pathology and clinical anatomy were not added beyond what the supplied revision-exercise answers and lecture slides actually name."*
     > *"Questions it asks that nothing on hand can verify — brachial plexus M-shape, femoral triangle borders, TMJ muscles — were left out."*

   - Marking unsourced anatomy (quadrangular space, Cloquet's node, cribriform fascia, regimental badge, etc.) as "app-authored" would misrepresent the app's mission. ## 4. Proposed Core Sourced Scope for Implementation (Upon Sign-off)

### `hss2011-upper-brachial-plexus-nerves`
- **Definition & Formation**: Network of intersecting nerves originating from the anterior (ventral) rami of C5–T1 spinal nerves (`hss.ul.2026` p15, `hss.4.3` p13).
- **Course**: Emerges from cervical spine, crosses over the First Rib behind the clavicle into the axilla (`hss.4.3` p14).
- **Five Terminal Branches & Functional Distribution**:
  1. *Musculocutaneous nerve*: anterior arm compartment flexors (biceps brachii, brachialis) (`hss.4.3` p24, `hss.ul.2026` p21).
  2. *Axillary nerve*: shoulder abduction via deltoid (`hss.4.3` p19, `hss.ul.2026` p28).
  3. *Radial nerve*: all extensors of elbow, wrist, and fingers (triceps brachii and posterior forearm extensor compartment) (`hss.4.3` p25, p27; `hss.ul.2026` p27).
  4. *Median nerve*: anterior forearm flexor compartment muscles (FCR, FDS, FPL, PQ, lateral FDP) and thenar muscles; passes through the carpal tunnel; compression causes Carpal Tunnel Syndrome (`hss.4.3` p27, p32; `hss.ul.2026` p23).
  5. *Ulnar nerve*: flexor carpi ulnaris, medial FDP, and intrinsic hand muscles (interossei, hypothenar) (`hss.4.3` p27; `hss.ul.2026` p23).
- **Citations**: `hss.ul.2026` p15, 21, 23, 27, 28; `hss.4.3` p13, 14, 15, 19, 24, 25, 27, 32.

### `hss2011-lower-femoral-triangle-vessels-nerves`
- **Location**: Anterior and superior aspect of the thigh (`hss.fib5yr` p19, `hss.ppans` p19).
- **Three Verified Boundaries**:
  1. *Superior boundary*: Inguinal ligament (`hss.fib5yr` p19, `hss.4.3` p54).
  2. *Medial boundary*: Adductor longus muscle (`hss.fib5yr` p19, `hss.4.3` p54).
  3. *Lateral boundary*: Sartorius muscle (`hss.fib5yr` p19, `hss.4.3` p54).
- **Three Verified Contents**:
  - *Femoral nerve* (lateral): innervates quadriceps femoris (knee extensor) and sartorius (`hss.4.3` p50, 51, 54; `hss.ll.2026` p33).
  - *Femoral artery* (intermediate): continuation of external iliac artery beneath inguinal ligament (`hss.3.3` p38, `hss.4.3` p54).
  - *Femoral vein* (medial): receives the great saphenous vein from medial leg/thigh and drains into the external iliac vein (`hss.3.3` p38, `hss.4.3` p54, `hss.manual1920` p77).
- **Lower Limb Vascular Continuity**:
  - Arterial line: External iliac $\rightarrow$ Femoral artery $\rightarrow$ Popliteal artery $\rightarrow$ Anterior and Posterior tibial arteries (`hss.manual1920` p77, `hss.revans` p5).
  - Venous return: Deep veins accompany arteries (popliteal, femoral, external iliac); superficial drainage via Great saphenous vein medially $\rightarrow$ Femoral vein (`hss.manual1920` p77, `hss.3.3` p38).
- **Citations**: `hss.4.3` p50, 51, 54; `hss.fib5yr` p19; `hss.ppans` p19, 22; `hss.3.3` p38; `hss.manual1920` p45, 77; `hss.revans` p5; `hss.ll.2026` p33.
