## Cut / deliberately out of scope

- **Sensory / motor homunculus fine detail** (hss.2.2 p16–19) — teach the concept inside the cortex item; do not build a standalone lesson. No question asks for the homunculus map.
- **Neurodegenerative disease detail** (hss.2.2 p22; hss.2.3 p22 Parkinson) — Alzheimer's / Parkinson's kept only as one clinical line each in the brainstem and cortex items (substantia nigra → dopamine → Parkinson is the only testable hook). No disease lesson.
- **Autonomic reflex-arc circuit diagrams and splanchnic-nerve routing** (hss.2.2 p26, p30–31) — beyond any question; the ANS item stops at sympathetic vs parasympathetic organisation and outflow.
- **Cerebellar histology — arbor vitae layers, Purkinje dendritic detail** (hss.2.2 p21) — "Purkinje cell" is the only tested token; keep it to a naming line.
- **Eye blood supply — ophthalmic / central retinal / infraorbital arteries** (hss.special.2017 p17–18) — not tested in any answer key; one line in the eyeball item at most.
- **MOOC "where/what/how" cortical-stream model and top-down filtering** (hss.mooc3 p24) — background framing for the stroke item, not its own lesson.
- **Blood–brain-barrier cellular mechanism / endothelial tight junctions** (hss.mooc3 p23) — the glia item states the astrocyte–BBB link (that is the tested fact) and stops.
- **Gustatory / auditory / vestibular central pathways past the thalamus** — named endpoint only (cortex lobe); no synaptic-relay lesson.
- **Extrinsic-eye-muscle actions as a movement drill** — folded into the accessory-eye item as innervation facts; not a standalone "eye movements" lesson (would breach the no-micro-lesson rule).

## Olfaction note

`hss.special.2017` has **no olfaction slide**. Its reading list (p2) points to Martini
pp.564–565,568 for "Olfactory Receptors, Olfactory Pathways, Olfactory Discrimination",
but pages 32–37 of the deck are gustation only — matching `outputs/study/corpus`'s
coverage note that the olfaction entry is a reading-list pointer, not taught content.

Olfactory **anatomy** therefore comes from `hss.2.3`:
- p33 — slide titled "Olfactory nerve (CN I)" (figure slide; establishes CN I as the olfactory nerve).
- p31 — cranial-nerve overview labels the **olfactory bulb (from CN I)** and **olfactory tract** on the base of the brain.
- p4 — functional-cortex slide labels the **primary olfactory cortex**.

So the teachable olfactory pathway from the confirmed decks is: olfactory epithelium →
olfactory nerve (CN I) → olfactory bulb → olfactory tract → primary olfactory cortex
(`hss.2.3` p4, p31, p33). No past-paper answer key (`hss.revans`, `hss.fib5yr`,
`hss.ppans`) contains an olfaction blank — smell is taught but effectively untested, so it
stays a short segment inside `hss2011-m2-taste-smell` rather than its own item.

## Gaps flagged (tested but no deck covers it well)

1. **Cerebral arterial supply / circle of Willis.** The "Mr LAW" stroke case is tested
   **every year** and turns on the **middle cerebral artery**; `hss.fib5yr`/`hss.ppans`
   p15,17 also test **vertebral arteries → basilar artery → posterior cerebral arterial
   circle**. None of `hss.2.2`, `hss.2.3` or `hss.special.2017` teaches cerebral
   circulation, and `hss.mooc3` ("Anatomical Correlates of Stroke") is actually about
   tracts, eyes/ears and glia — it has **no arterial-territory map**. The existing
   `hss2011-cns-ventricles-csf-blood` title promises "cerebral arterial circle" but the
   deck pages it can cite (`hss.2.3` p27–30) do not support it. Writing
   `hss2011-m2-stroke-correlates` will need the two past-paper sentences as its only
   in-scope anchor for the arterial content, or a sourced substitute deck must be found
   before that part of the lesson can be written to the source-traceability rule.

   **RESOLVED (commit 83aee73):** `MOOC 2 — Arterial Supply and Venous Drainage of Neck
   and Head, and Meninges` is now registered as `hss.mooc2` (16 pages, in
   `source-text.json`). It teaches the whole of it: internal carotid → ACA (frontal/
   parietal) / MCA (lateral hemispheres), vertebral → basilar, the cerebral arterial
   circle with its communicating arteries (`hss.mooc2` p5), stroke → L/R MCA aphasia and
   contralateral paralysis (p5), the dural venous sinuses (p8) and the three meningeal
   layers with their spaces (p10–11). `hss2011-m2-stroke-correlates` and the meninges
   part of `hss2011-cns-spinal-cord-meninges` now cite `hss.mooc2`.
2. **"Central pattern generators"** (`hss.revans` FIB 2.1) — the term is a model answer
   but appears on none of the cached deck pages. The reflex/locomotion item can name it
   only if a supporting slide or past-paper stem is located; otherwise tag it an
   app-authored bridge from the spinal-cord tract material.
3. **Neuron/glia classification** is tested hard (astrocytes/BBB every year) but lives
   only in `hss.mooc3` p23 — there is no lecture-slide treatment in `hss.2.2`/`hss.2.3`
