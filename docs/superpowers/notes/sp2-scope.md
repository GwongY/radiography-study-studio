## Existing Items in Scope (15 Preserved) + Proposed New Items (6)

### Week 8 — Cardiovascular & Lymphatics (8 items)
1. `hss2011-m1-heart-wall-valves` (existing): Pericardium (fibrous vs serous: parietal/visceral/epicardium, pericardial cavity & fluid), heart wall 3 layers, cardiac muscle histology (striations, branched cells, intercalated discs with gap junctions and desmosomes).
2. `hss2011-cvs-internal-chambers-valves` (existing): Sectional anatomy, 4 chambers, interatrial/interventricular septa, fossa ovalis, pectinate muscles, trabeculae carneae, moderator band; AV valves (tricuspid 3 cusps, mitral/bicuspid 2 cusps) with chordae tendineae and papillary muscles; semilunar valves (aortic, pulmonary 3 pocket cusps) and fibrous skeleton.
3. `hss2011-cvs-coronary-circulation-conduction` (existing): Coronary circulation: left coronary artery (LAD/anterior interventricular and circumflex) and right coronary artery (marginal and posterior interventricular); cardiac veins (great, middle, small) draining into coronary sinus; intrinsic conduction system (SA node pacemaker, AV node 100 ms delay, AV bundle of His, bundle branches, Purkinje fibres).
4. **NEW:** `hss2011-cvs-blood-vessel-circuits`: Systemic vs pulmonary circuits; blood vessel histology: three concentric tunics (tunica intima with endothelium and internal elastic membrane, tunica media with smooth muscle and elastic fibres, tunica externa/adventitia); classification of arteries (elastic, muscular, arterioles), capillaries (continuous, fenestrated, sinusoid), and veins (valves, capacitance vessels).
5. **NEW:** `hss2011-cvs-lymphatic-system`: Lymphatic system overview: lymph fluid, lymphatic capillaries (overlapping endothelial flaps, minivalves, lacteals), collecting vessels, lymphatic trunks; thoracic duct (drains left side of head/neck/thorax, left upper limb, and entire lower body below diaphragm via cisterna chyli into left subclavian/internal jugular junction) vs right lymphatic duct (drains right upper quadrant into right subclavian/internal jugular junction); lymph node microarchitecture (capsule, trabeculae, subcapsular sinus, cortex with germinal centres, medulla, afferent vs efferent vessels).
6. `hss2011-cvs-tutorial-pastpaper-practice` (existing): Authentic HKPolyU past-paper synthesis for CVS: chamber wall thickness comparison, valve auscultation mechanics, anterior interventricular sulcus landmarks, cisterna chyli lymphatic drainage MCQ/FIB.
7. `hss2011-structures-heartChambers` (existing 3D companion): Dynamic studio lesson inspecting right atrium, right ventricle, left atrium, left ventricle, tricuspid, mitral, and pulmonary valves on the 3D circulatory model.
8. `hss2011-structures-greatVessels` (existing 3D companion): Dynamic studio lesson inspecting SVC, IVC, pulmonary trunk, right/left pulmonary arteries, aortic arch, coronary sinus, and RCA on the 3D circulatory model.

### Week 9 — Respiratory System (7 items)
1. `hss2011-resp-upper-tract-larynx` (existing): Upper respiratory tract: nasal cavity boundaries (nasal bones, maxillae, frontal, sphenoid, ethmoid, vomer), nasal septum, superior/middle/inferior conchae and meatuses; 4 paired paranasal sinuses (frontal, sphenoid, ethmoid, maxillary); pharynx (nasopharynx, oropharynx, laryngopharynx); larynx cartilages (3 large unpaired: thyroid, cricoid, epiglottis; 3 paired: arytenoid, corniculate, cuneiform), thyrohyoid ligament, vocal folds and glottis.
2. `hss2011-m1-lungs-airway` (existing): Trachea (length 11 cm, diameter 2.5 cm, extends C6 to T5 carina, 15–20 C-shaped hyaline rings, trachealis muscle posteriorly); bronchial tree branching: right primary bronchus (shorter, wider, more vertical -> aspiration risk) vs left primary bronchus; secondary (lobar) bronchi (3 right, 2 left); tertiary (segmental) bronchi (10 right, 8–10 left); conducting vs terminal vs respiratory bronchioles.
3. **NEW:** `hss2011-resp-respiratory-epithelium-alveoli`: Respiratory mucosa histology: pseudostratified ciliated columnar epithelium with goblet cells (mucus escalator defense against particles >10 µm); transition to stratified squamous in oropharynx/laryngopharynx, cuboidal in bronchioles, and simple squamous in alveoli; pulmonary lobule architecture; alveolar cells: Type I pneumocytes (95% alveolar surface, gas diffusion), Type II pneumocytes (secrete pulmonary surfactant to reduce surface tension and prevent alveolar collapse), and alveolar macrophages (dust cells); three-layer respiratory membrane / blood-air barrier (<0.5 µm: alveolar squamous epithelium, fused basal lamina, capillary endothelial cell).
4. `hss2011-resp-pleura-lungs-gross` (existing): Gross lung anatomy: apex (extends superior to 1st rib into neck root) vs base (rests on diaphragm); lobes and fissures (right lung: superior, middle, inferior separated by horizontal and oblique fissures; left lung: superior and inferior separated by oblique fissure, cardiac notch and lingula); medial surface and pulmonary hilum / root (pulmonary artery superior, pulmonary veins inferior/anterior, main bronchus posterior); pleural membranes (visceral vs parietal), pleural cavity with serous fluid, costodiaphragmatic and costomediastinal recesses; dual blood supply (pulmonary arteries for gas exchange vs bronchial arteries from descending aorta for lung tissue nourishment).
5. **NEW:** `hss2011-resp-mechanics-muscles-breathing`: Mechanics of pulmonary ventilation: Boyle's law ($P \propto 1/V$); quiet inspiration (active: diaphragm contracting downward [phrenic nerve C3–C5] + external intercostals elevating ribs [bucket-handle & water-pump handle motion]); quiet expiration (passive elastic recoil of lungs and chest wall); forced inspiration (accessory muscles: sternocleidomastoid, scalenes, pectoralis minor, serratus anterior); forced expiration (active: internal intercostals, abdominal wall muscles: rectus abdominis, external/internal obliques, transversus abdominis depressing ribs and pushing diaphragm upward).
6. `hss2011-resp-tutorial-pastpaper-practice` (existing): Authentic HKPolyU past-paper synthesis for Respiratory: sequential airflow pathway (nostrils -> pharynx -> larynx -> trachea -> bronchial tree -> alveoli), epithelial transitions, bronchial artery origin, and foreign body aspiration mechanics.
7. `hss2011-structures-airwayTree` (existing 3D companion): Dynamic studio lesson inspecting trachea, carina, right/left main bronchi, lobar bronchi, lung lobes, and pleura on the 3D respiratory/organ model.

### Week 10 — Regional Anatomy of the Thorax (6 items)
1. `hss2011-osteo-ribs-sternum` (existing, in `outputs/study/corpus/hss-osteology.js`): Osteology of the thoracic cage: sternum (manubrium, body, xiphoid process; jugular/suprasternal notch; sternal angle of Louis at T4/T5 marking 2nd costal cartilage, aortic arch, carina, and transverse thoracic plane); 12 pairs of ribs (true ribs 1–7 attaching directly via costal cartilage, false ribs 8–10 attaching via 7th cartilage, floating ribs 11–12 with no anterior attachment); anatomy of a typical rib (head with two facets, neck, tubercle with articular facet, costal angle, costal groove on inferior border sheltering intercostal neurovascular bundle: vein, artery, nerve [VAN top-to-bottom]); costovertebral and costotransverse joints; typical thoracic vertebrae features.
2. `hss2011-thorax-intercostal-diaphragm` (existing): Boundaries of the thorax: thoracic inlet / superior thoracic aperture (manubrium sterni, 1st pair of ribs & cartilages, T1 body; clavicle is NOT part of inlet); thoracic outlet / inferior thoracic aperture (xiphoid process, costal margin, 12th ribs, T12 body); the diaphragm: domes, central tendon, right and left crura (right crus L1–L3 forms esophageal loop, left crus L1–L2); three major diaphragmatic apertures: caval opening at T8 (IVC, right phrenic nerve), esophageal hiatus at T10 (esophagus, vagal trunks, left gastric vessels), aortic hiatus at T12 (aorta, thoracic duct, azygos vein); mnemonic "I8 10EGGs At12"; innervation by phrenic nerve (C3, C4, C5 keeps the diaphragm alive); motor vs sensory innervation (phrenic central, intercostal peripheral).
3. **NEW:** `hss2011-thorax-mediastinum-divisions-contents`: Mediastinum boundaries and compartmentalization: anteriorly sternum, posteriorly thoracic vertebrae, superiorly thoracic inlet, inferiorly diaphragm, laterally mediastinal pleura; horizontal transverse thoracic plane (sternal angle to T4/T5 disc) dividing superior from inferior mediastinum; Superior mediastinum contents (thymus remnants, brachiocephalic veins, SVC, aortic arch and 3 branches, trachea, esophagus, thoracic duct, vagus and phrenic nerves, left recurrent laryngeal nerve); Inferior mediastinum sub-divisions: Anterior mediastinum (thymus remnants, internal thoracic lymph nodes, fat/areolar tissue), Middle mediastinum (pericardial sac, heart, ascending aorta, pulmonary trunk, termination of SVC/IVC, phrenic nerves), Posterior mediastinum (esophagus, descending thoracic aorta and branches, azygos and hemiazygos veins, thoracic duct, vagal nerves, sympathetic trunks, greater/lesser/least splanchnic nerves).
4. `hss2011-thorax-regional-landmarks` (existing): Surface anatomy & clinical projection landmarks: Surface projections of heart valves and auscultation sites: Aortic valve (2nd right intercostal space at right sternal border), Pulmonary valve (2nd left intercostal space at left sternal border), Tricuspid valve (5th left intercostal space at lower left sternal border), Mitral/bicuspid valve (5th left intercostal space at midclavicular line / apex beat; "All Patients Take Meds"); Surface projections of lungs vs pleura: midclavicular line (lung rib 6, pleura rib 8), midaxillary line (lung rib 8, pleura rib 10), paravertebral/scapular line (lung rib 10, pleura rib 12); costodiaphragmatic recess (potential space 2 ribs deep below lung base, site of pleural effusion and thoracentesis); needle insertion safety rule: insert immediately superior to rib to avoid VAN bundle running in costal groove on inferior border.
5. **NEW:** `hss2011-thorax-breast-axillary-lymphatics`: Mammary gland (breast) anatomy and clinical lymphatic drainage: position on anterior chest wall (ribs 2–6 from sternum to midaxillary line, resting on pectoralis major and serratus anterior fascia); axillary tail of Spence extending superolaterally through deep fascia into axilla; internal structure: 15–20 glandular lobes, lactiferous ducts, lactiferous sinuses (ampullae) under nipple-areola complex, suspensory ligaments of Cooper (fibrous connective tissue septa connecting skin to deep fascia; dimpling in malignancy); arterial supply: internal thoracic (internal mammary) artery, lateral thoracic artery, intercostal arteries; venous drainage into axillary and internal thoracic veins; lymphatic drainage pathways: ~75% drains laterally to axillary lymph node groups (pectoral/anterior, subscapular/posterior, humeral/lateral -> central -> apical -> supraclavicular/subclavian trunk), ~20–25% drains medially to parasternal (internal thoracic) nodes (pathway for contralateral metastasis), and inferiorly to subdiaphragmatic/hepatic lymphatics.
6. `hss2011-thorax-tutorial-pastpaper-practice` (existing): Authentic HKPolyU past-paper synthesis for Thorax: thoracic inlet boundary exclusions (clavicle/scapula), aortic arch location in superior mediastinum, esophageal descent through superior and posterior mediastinum, diaphragm aperture vertebral levels (T8, T10, T12), internal thoracic artery mammary supply, and posterior intercostal / azygos venous drainage.
