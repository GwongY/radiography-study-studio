## Cut / Deliberately Out of Scope

- **Detailed histology of bone cell biochemical signaling / RANKL / osteoclast brush borders**: Keep to cell definitions and functions; detailed molecular cascades are ABCT/pathology scope.
- **Deep intrinsic hand muscles (interossei, lumbricals, thenar/hypothenar fine origins)**: General carpal tunnel and palmar/dorsal grouping taught; individual deep hand muscle origins/insertions are not in the lecture deck or exam keys.
- **Deep intrinsic foot muscles (four individual plantar layers)**: General arches of foot, plantar aponeurosis and primary compartment tendons taught; four individual intrinsic plantar layers are not examined in Year 1 Sem 1.
- **Complex facial muscular variants and modiolus biomechanics**: Folded into major facial expression actions; no standalone micro-lesson.
- **Carotid triangle / submandibular triangle fine boundary drill**: Taught inside neck gross anatomy; no standalone surgical triangle lesson.
